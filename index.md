Hello! My name is **Melvyn Huynh**, and I like building robots that learn from people. I'm a second-year M.Sc. student in [Robotics at EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/education/master/programs/robotics/), where I also earned my B.Sc. in Microengineering.

I'm interested in embodied AI, machine learning and human-robot interaction: how a robot can pick up a skill by learning from data, and how it can then work naturally alongside people.

I'm currently a research assistant at the [Applied AI Lab (AAIL)](https://wp.unil.ch/aail/), where I build a Reddit dataset on how solopreneurs experience AI, and a teaching assistant for the AI for Business course. I'm also doing a semester project on human-robot interaction at [Idiap](https://www.idiap.ch/en), building a vision-based module that lets the Pepper robot infer user engagement.

At [N-Pulse](https://make.epfl.ch/projects/60/make-epfl-n-pulse-60), I first worked on firmware, integrating AI models on an STM32 to decode hand movements from EEG and EMG signals. I'm now working on computer vision, deploying a multi-class object detection model on an Arduino UNO Q so that a prosthetic arm can grab objects.

Previously, I worked on imitation learning with the [EPFL AI Team](https://epflaiteam.ch/projects/dexter), using LeRobot with a leader arm and a follower arm ([SO-101](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100)) to collect demonstrations and train manipulation policies.

Outside of robotics, I play piano, and I care a lot about my connection to Vietnam and France. There's more on that on my [Personal](/personal) page.

Shoot me an email if you want to chat about robots, piano, life, or anything really! 🧧
